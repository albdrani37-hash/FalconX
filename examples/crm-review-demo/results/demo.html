Evidence & limits

Updates and audit entries commit together in SQLite. Exact approved IDs are tied to a content-derived plan identifier. Changed source rows abort a pending batch. Repeating an applied batch creates no additional writes.

This demonstrates local mechanics only. It has no authenticated reviewer identity, external CRM integration, authorization server, encrypted storage, semantic deduplication, live LLM classification or production performance claim. Local administrators can change SQLite; this is not a tamper-proof audit system.
