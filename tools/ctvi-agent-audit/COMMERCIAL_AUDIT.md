# AI Agent Trace Reliability & Evidence Audit

Abdullah Alharbi — independent researcher and AI technology developer, Saudi Arabia. Alharbi AI Research & Solutions is a working brand, not a verified registered legal entity.

## When this service is useful

For agent developers and implementation teams that need to understand missing execution records, broken tool-call/result links, unsupported evidence references, and what an exported trace cannot establish. The engagement begins with one agreed workflow and a sample the customer is authorized to share.

## Defined initial engagement

- One agreed trace format and workflow; up to 100 redacted traces and 10,000 total events, subject to a sample-based feasibility check.
- Explicit source-to-normalized-schema mapping with documented omissions, rather than assuming provider exports are interchangeable.
- Reproducible structural checks and an evidence-linked findings report, including unknowns and observed failures.
- A prioritized remediation list and one rerun against an agreed corrected sample.
- Installation/run instructions and a review call or written walkthrough.

The customer provides the intended outcome, schema or sample, relevant system boundary, permission to process the records, and acceptance examples. Use pseudonymized structural records; omit credentials, personal prose, health/payment data and unpublished third-party information. Agree a transfer method and retention/deletion procedure before exchanging customer data. Do not email raw production logs.

## Acceptance criteria

The agreed sample parses or receives a documented rejection; mapping losses and unsupported fields are recorded; every reported finding identifies a reproducible check and record position; unknown evidence remains unknown; the same frozen input produces the same technical result in the documented environment. Acceptance is based on these deliverables, not a promise to discover defects or certify safety.
